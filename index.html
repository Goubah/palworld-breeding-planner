About this breeding calculator

Most Palworld breeding calculators answer “what do these two Pals make?” or “which pairs produce this Pal?”. This one starts from the opposite end: you enter the Pals you already own, along with their genders and passive skills, then name a target Pal and up to four passive skills you want it to end up with. It searches multi-generation breeding routes that begin only from your roster — no assuming you can go catch a perfect parent.

Passive skills are part of the search, not an afterthought

Passive skill inheritance is modelled on the game’s own numbers: a child draws its passives from the combined pool of both parents on a 40/30/20/10 weighting, with random passives filling any remaining slots. Every step of a route shows the real chance per egg, how many eggs you should expect to hatch, and a rough time estimate, so you can tell a one-hour plan from a twenty-hour one before committing to it.

Gender ratios and Pal Reversers

Breeding needs one male and one female, and many species are heavily gender-skewed, so gender is priced into every step rather than ignored. Where a pairing only works by flipping a Pal’s gender with a Pal Reverser, the route says so explicitly instead of quietly pretending the pairing is free.

Runs entirely in your browser

The breeding table is derived from the game’s own data files. Everything — your roster and the whole search — stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to make. Results come from a heuristic search, so they are good routes rather than proven-optimal ones.
