About this breeding calculator

Most Palworld breeding calculators answer “what do these two Pals make?” or “which pairs produce this Pal?”. This one works the other way round. You enter the Pals you already own, with their genders and passive skills, then name a target Pal and up to four passive skills you want on it. It searches multi-generation breeding routes that start from your roster only. There is no fallback that assumes you can go and catch a better parent.

Passive skills

Passive inheritance uses the game’s own numbers. A child draws its passives from the combined pool of both parents on a 40/30/20/10 weighting, and random passives fill any slots left over. Every step of a route shows the chance per egg, how many eggs you should expect to hatch, and a rough time estimate. That way you can tell a one-hour plan from a twenty-hour one before you start.

Gender ratios and Pal Reversers

Breeding needs one male and one female, and many species are heavily gender-skewed, so gender is part of the cost of every step. Where a pairing only works by flipping a Pal’s gender with a Pal Reverser, the route tells you which Pal needs it.

Runs entirely in your browser

The breeding table is derived from the game’s own data files. Your roster and the whole search stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account to make. Route finding is a heuristic search, so it finds good routes, but not necessarily the best one that exists.

Heuristic beam search over a game-derived breeding table — results are good routes, not proven-optimal ones. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
